Help

Is this mining?

No. SatsNest is a rewards site. Nothing is mined. You claim small amounts of crypto on a timer.

How do I earn?

Open the Earn tab and claim each coin once an hour. Your balance goes up with every claim.

What is a satoshi?

A satoshi, or sat, is the smallest piece of a Bitcoin: 0.00000001 BTC.

How do I withdraw?

Open the Wallet tab, choose a coin, paste your own wallet address, enter an amount, and tap Request withdrawal.

Do I have to buy anything?

No. Everything on SatsNest is free, and you can withdraw what you earn without buying anything.

What is the minimum withdrawal?

Each coin has a minimum. It is shown on the Wallet tab under the amount box.

How long does a payout take?

Payouts are sent by hand, so it can take a while. Your request shows as pending until it is sent, then as paid.

I used a wrong address. Can I get the coins back?

No. Crypto sent to the wrong address can’t be recovered, so check the address before you send the request.
